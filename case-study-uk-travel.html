The engagement

The brand ran three sister sites — an established flagship plus two newer properties. My scope was off-page across all three: build quality, relevant referring domains and lift domain authority brand-wide. Every placement was vetted to a minimum Domain Rating of 15 in Ahrefs, and I stripped the sites' cross-links to each other from the results, since a link from a site the same company owns isn't earned authority.

Property 1 — Established flagship

The oldest domain, already carrying authority when the engagement began.

Estimated organic traffic · monthly

Traffic +77% (1,053 → 1,868/mo) · DR ~22 → ~26 · +45 referring domains · 68 live backlinks. Already an established domain, so gains showed up more in traffic than raw authority score. Total keyword count held steady while priority target keywords were driven into the top 1–3 — gains concentrated in the positions that convert.

Property 2 — Biggest mover

A newer property where the off-page build translated into the strongest growth.

Estimated organic traffic · monthly

Traffic +255% (315 → 1,119/mo) · DR 11 → 16 · 44 referring domains · 173 live backlinks. The strongest result of the three: a higher-quality earned link profile paired with real keyword growth — tracked keywords rose 31% and top-3 rankings doubled.

Property 3 — Newest site, authority focus

The smallest, newest property — here the work was foundational authority-building.

DR 9 → 16 · 8 referring domains · 5 live backlinks. The largest Domain Rating lift of the three — nearly doubling authority on a site starting from a low base. Organic traffic on this smaller property stayed volatile and didn't establish a clear trend; the goal here was to build the foundational backlink profile, and DR growth reflects that.

The takeaway

Across the three properties I built 93 unique referring domains (246 live backlinks), lifting Domain Rating on every site and helping combined organic traffic grow roughly 45% over the engagement — concentrated in the two properties where authority gains translated into search demand.

Client anonymized under NDA. Referring domains are deduplicated across the three sites and exclude the sites' links to each other. Traffic figures are Ahrefs / SEMrush estimates; one property showed a one-off traffic anomaly that is not treated as a result.
