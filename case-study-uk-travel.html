UK Travel Brand: Three-Site Portfolio

The challenge

The client was a UK travel brand with three sister sites: one older main site and two newer ones. The brand was known, but all three sites had very few backlinks. The main site had only 3 referring domains. The second site had 14, and most were very weak (median authority score of 2). The newest site had none. So this wasn't about protecting rankings or cleaning up bad links. It was about building real links for three sites from almost zero, without the easy shortcut of linking the sites to each other and calling it authority.

I worked on this alone. A UK-based SEO consultant assigned and checked my work, and handled the client.

The plan

Keep it simple. Check every site against the DR 15 minimum so no weak links got in. Use a mix of link types so the links looked natural, not mass-built. Give the newest site the most attention, since it was starting from nothing, while building up the other two.

What I did

I got links in four ways: directory and citation listings, guest posts, HARO-style expert quotes, and roundup contributions. I checked each site in Ahrefs against the DR 15 minimum before going after it. For pages that needed to rank, I also wrote content briefs for the writer (topic, meta title and description, keywords, and internal links), so new pages could make the most of the new links.

Because these are sister sites, I removed every link between them before reporting, since links between sites the same company owns aren't earned. I also counted each referring domain once across all three sites, so a directory that lists all three counts as one, not three.

Property 1: Established flagship

The oldest site, which already had some authority when I started.

Estimated organic traffic · monthly
Traffic rose from 1,053 to 1,868 per month.

Traffic +77% (1,053 to 1,868/mo) · DR ~22 to ~26 · 3 to 45 referring domains · 68 live backlinks. Since this site was already established, the gains showed more in traffic than in Domain Rating. The total number of keywords stayed about the same, but the main target keywords moved into positions 1 to 3, which is where the clicks and sales come from.

Property 2: Biggest mover

A newer site, and the one that grew the most.

Estimated organic traffic · monthly
Traffic rose from 315 to 1,119 per month.

Traffic +255% (315 to 1,119/mo) · DR 11 to 16 · 14 to 44 referring domains · 173 live backlinks. The best result of the three: better links plus real keyword growth. Tracked keywords went up 31% and the number of top-3 rankings doubled.

Property 3: Newest site, authority focus

The smallest and newest site. Here the goal was to build its first real backlinks.

DR 9 to 16 · 0 to 8 referring domains · 5 live backlinks. The biggest Domain Rating jump of the three, almost doubling from a low start. Traffic on this small site went up and down with no clear trend, including a one-time spike in July that I don't count as a result. The goal here was building the first backlinks, and the DR growth shows that.

What I learned

Three things. First, the same approach doesn't give the same results everywhere: it got one site +255% traffic and left another flat, because site age, size, and content matter as much as links. Second, traffic estimates for small sites aren't reliable, so for those I focus on Domain Rating and referring domains instead. Third, pairing links with content briefs helped: the site with pages built around target keywords and internal links grew the most.
