The brief

A UK travel tour operator with a nearly non-existent backlink profile needed to build search authority from the ground up. My scope was off-page: grow the referring-domain profile with quality, relevant links, and support the wider effort where it touched link equity.

What I did

Over the engagement I built the referring-domain profile from near-zero to 45 domains (68 live backlinks), through directory, citation, and guest-post outreach — with every placement vetted to a minimum Domain Rating of 15 in Ahrefs. Alongside the link work, I directed blog content with a writer and guided internal linking so new pages earned and passed authority effectively.

The result

Estimated organic traffic grew roughly 77% over the engagement, climbing from about 1,050 to 1,870 visits per month, and Domain Rating rose from 11 to around 20. Notably, this happened while the total tracked keyword count held steady — the gains concentrated in the priority target keywords I drove into the top 1–3, the positions that actually convert to traffic rather than raw keyword volume.

Client anonymized under NDA. Traffic figures are Ahrefs / SEMrush estimates.
