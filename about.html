About

Hi, I'm John.

I'm an SEO specialist with 3+ years in link building and digital PR. Now I work on all three parts of SEO: off-page, on-page, and technical, plus SEO content writing.

Off-page is where I have the most experience. That's link building, growing referring domains, directory and citation listings, and editorial outreach. From September 2023 to August 2025, I did link building as an independent contractor, including resource-page link building for a UK marketing agency's clients in jewellery, travel, finance, and home retail. From March to November 2025, I ran link building and digital PR for 8 clients at once under a UK-based SEO consultant, in eCommerce, travel, restaurants, manufacturing, and B2B services. Then from November 2025 to May 2026, I freelanced, doing backlink audits, link building plans, and HARO, Qwoted, and Featured outreach.

Since June 2026, I've worked in-house at KDCI Outsourcing as an Off-Page SEO Specialist. Besides link building, I also handle on-page SEO, technical SEO, and content writing. On-page, I map keywords to pages, fix titles, meta descriptions, and headers, and add internal links and alt text. On the technical side, I run site audits in Screaming Frog and Semrush, check page speed and Core Web Vitals, manage sitemaps, robots.txt, and schema, and I'm leading a site move from Webflow to Replit, including the redirect mapping and the checks after launch. I also write and publish SEO blog posts and help our content writers with keywords and on-page SEO.

I keep my numbers honest. I only count links from the dates I actually worked on a project. I remove links between sister sites, set aside low-value links, mark Domain Rating as approximate, and say so when something didn't work. I'd rather show a smaller number I can prove than a bigger one I can't.

I always start with an audit: the site, its pages, and its backlinks. Then I check what's working for the competitors ranking above it. I mostly work in Ahrefs, Semrush, and Screaming Frog, and I don't use shortcuts like link farms that stop working after the next Google update.

How I work

Check first, then fix, then show the results.

01 · Audit & analysis

Find where the gaps are

Crawl the site for technical and on-page problems, and check its backlinks for gaps and spammy links. Then look at the competitors ranking above it: which sites link to them, and which keywords they rank for that the client doesn't. This tells me where to focus before any outreach starts.

02 · Strategy

Make the plan

Turn what I found into a plan: which technical fixes come first, which keyword goes on which page, the internal links, and the content worth building links to. I also set the minimum Domain Rating a site needs before I'll go after a link from it.

03 · Execution

Do the work

Fix the technical and on-page issues. Build the list of sites to reach out to, check it, send the outreach, and negotiate when a project has a link budget. I track every link I get so the reports are accurate and nothing is counted twice.

04 · Reporting

Report what changed

Weekly and monthly reports: what I did, what links I got, and how rankings, traffic, site health, referring domains, and Domain Rating changed. I include what didn't work too, so the next month's plan is based on real results.
