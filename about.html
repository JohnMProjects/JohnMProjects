About

Hi, I'm John.

I'm an off-page SEO specialist with a focus on the part of search that's hardest to fake: authority.

Over the past few years I've built and managed backlink profiles for brands across outsourcing, AI staffing, SaaS, and local services, running directory and editorial link campaigns, cleaning up messy referring-domain profiles, and tracking the metrics that actually move rankings. My work lives in referring domains, Authority Score, and the quality of every link that points back to a site.

I care about the durable version of SEO. No link farms, no shortcuts that unravel after the next algorithm update, just steady, defensible growth in the signals search engines trust.

When I'm not prospecting links or auditing backlink profiles, I'm refining the workflows and tools that make the work faster and sharper.

On projects that call for it, I also brief the content side: writing tasks for the writer that spell out the target topic, meta title and description, keywords to target, and the internal linking structure, so new content is built to earn and pass authority from day one.
