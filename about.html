About

Hi, I'm John.

I'm an off-page SEO specialist. I work on the part of search that's hardest to fake and slowest to build: authority.

That means link building, referring-domain growth, directory and citation work, and the editorial outreach that earns the kind of links other people pay for. Most of what's in this portfolio is client work delivered two ways: solo engagements assigned by a UK-based SEO consultant, and resource-page campaigns run as part of a Philippine delivery team for a UK marketing agency. Across those I've worked with brands in travel, CBD, luxury jewellery, home retail, personal finance, and credit cards, building clean referring-domain profiles, defending competitive commercial rankings, and standing up brand-new domains from zero.

What I lead with is honesty, because the field is short on it. Every number on this site is filtered to what I can defend: engagement windows only, sister-site cross-links stripped, low-value and automated links set aside, Domain Rating marked approximate. Where a result was thin or a lane didn't win, I say so on the page. I would rather show a smaller number I can prove than a bigger one I can't.

Before I prospect a single link, I audit: the backlink profile for gaps, then competitor backlink and keyword gap analysis to see exactly what's already lifting the sites outranking the client, so I can reverse-engineer it and add more. I work across Ahrefs and Semrush depending on what a client tracks, and I care about the durable version of SEO. No link farms, no shortcuts that unravel after the next update, just steady, defensible growth in the signals search engines actually trust.

On projects that call for it, I brief the content side too: the target topic, meta title and description, keyword focus, and internal linking, so new pages are built to earn and pass authority from day one.

How I work

Audit first, then earn, then prove it.

01 · Audit & analysis

Find where the authority gap is

Vet the site and audit its backlink profile for gaps and toxic patterns. Then run competitor backlink and keyword gap analysis to see exactly what's lifting the sites already outranking the client, and study their keywords and pages against it. This is the part that decides where to aim before a single email goes out.

02 · Strategy

Reverse-engineer the plan

Turn the gaps into a plan: the linkable assets worth building, the internal linking, and any easy-win keywords worth layering in. Set the client's required Domain Rating floor so every prospect is measured against it from the start.

03 · Execution

Prospect, QA, outreach

Build the prospect list against that floor, QA it, run outreach, and negotiate where a project has a link budget. Keep clean data on every placement so the reporting is accurate and nothing is double-counted.

04 · Reporting

Show what actually moved

Weekly and monthly: what got done, what landed, and how the metrics moved, referring domains, Domain Rating, and rankings. Honest about what worked and what didn't, so the next month's plan is built on real signal.
