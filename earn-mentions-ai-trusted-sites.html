How to earn mentions on the sites AI actually trusts

By John Masajo · Published August 3, 2026 · 3 min read

If AI answers are built from sources the AI trusts, then off-page work has a clearer target than before. The job isn't just earning links anymore. It's getting your brand onto the specific sites AI relies on when it answers questions in your niche.

What the pattern looks like

Semrush looked at how brands earn visibility in AI answers and found a clear pattern. A brand like Patagonia gets tens of thousands of AI mentions, and most of them don't come from its own website. They come from third-party sites, community platforms, review sites, and retailers that talk about it. AI trusts what several sources agree on. When a few independent, respected sites say the same thing about a brand, the AI treats that brand as a safe answer.

How prospecting changes

Normal link prospecting asks one question: which sites have authority and relevance in my space? Now there's a second question: which sites does AI already cite when it answers questions in my category? Those sites go to the top of your list, because a mention there does two jobs. Real people read it, and the AI reads it too.

The easiest way to find them

Ask the AI. Take the real questions your customers ask, run them through ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Mode, and write down which sources get cited. That list is your target list, and it's based on what the AI actually does, not a guess. Do this for about a dozen main questions and you'll see the same sites come up again and again. Focus on those.

What to go after on those sites

Not just links. Depending on the site, the win might be an honest review, a spot in a comparison or roundup, an expert quote, or a real presence in a community discussion. What matters is that independent sources back you up, because that's what builds trust with AI. A link is still great, but the mention itself counts now too.

Don't fake it

Earn it. Google's 2026 guidance clearly warns against planting fake mentions on forums and blogs, and fake reviews or planted posts get ignored and can hurt you. The mentions that help are the ones you really earned on sites that are already trusted. Faking it is the fastest way to lose it.

The prospecting question changed from "who will link to me?" to "which sites does AI trust, and how do I earn a real spot on them?" Answer that honestly, and you start showing up where the answers get written.

Earning a place on sites that people and AI trust is what my portfolio is built on.
