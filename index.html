How I report

Every figure is filtered before you see it.

The reason the numbers above are smaller than what most agencies quote is that they are what is left after the filtering. Here is what that means.

Windowed to the engagement

Only links earned inside the dates I actually worked count. Nothing before, nothing after.

Deduped and cleaned

Sister-site cross-links and auto-generated links are stripped out. One domain, counted once.

Vetted to a DR floor

Every placement is measured against the client's required Domain Rating before it counts.

Shortfalls called out

Where a lane did not win, the case study says so. I would rather show a smaller number I can prove.
